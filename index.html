Questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? Email the team.

Which networks does Felix listen to?

LinkedIn, X, YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, plus anything with an RSS feed: Substack, Medium, Hacker News, Product Hunt, podcasts, news sites and blogs. You can track keywords, specific accounts, competitors and mentions of your brand.

Can Felix reply on Substack, podcasts or news sites?

No. Feeds are listen-only. Felix reads them for competitor moves, brand mentions and trends, then turns what it finds into alerts and post ideas you can publish on your own social accounts.

Does Felix post or comment without asking me?

No. Every reply and post waits for approval by default. You can approve in the app, in Slack or by email, and agencies can send approval links to clients.

How does it learn my voice?

Felix reads your past posts and builds a voice profile: tone, sentence length, words you use and words you avoid. Drafts are written against that profile and get better as you edit them.

How is this different from Flywheel?

Felix is the listening and engagement side of Flywheel, packaged on its own. If you already use Flywheel, Felix sits alongside your existing calendar and voice profiles.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade any time and the change applies from your next billing period.
